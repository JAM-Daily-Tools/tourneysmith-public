## Apple license and contact details

No custom EULA will be submitted to Apple for the initial launch. Apple’s
standard licensed-application EULA governs the iOS app license, while the
TourneySmith Terms govern the account, hosted service, user content, and
conduct. This avoids publishing a telephone number solely to satisfy Apple’s
custom-EULA minimum terms.

If the app later enters EU App Store territories as a trader, Apple requires a
public address, telephone number, and email address on the product page. A
dedicated business telephone number must be selected before that expansion.

Primary source:
https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements

## EEA and UK expansion gate

Before intentionally offering TourneySmith in the EEA or United Kingdom:

1. Appoint an EEA Article 27 representative and a separate UK representative
   where required.
2. Add the exact appointed entities and contact details to every policy
   translation.
3. Complete Apple’s EU trader verification with a public business telephone
   number, email address, and verified company address.
4. Review Google and other provider transfer documentation for the final
   production account.
5. Obtain qualified EEA, UK, French, Italian, and Spain legal review.
6. Change the availability language only after the storefront settings match.

Published representative pricing researched on 2026-09-27 ranged from about
EUR 150 per year for a small, low-risk account to EUR 390 per year for a basic
combined EEA and UK appointment, with broader services costing more. Pricing is
volatile and must be rechecked. No provider has been selected or appointed.
