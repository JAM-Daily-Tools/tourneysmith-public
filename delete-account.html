Delete your TourneySmith account

Last updated: September 27, 2026. Document version: 2026-09-27.

Before you delete

Account deletion cannot be fully undone. Export is handled by request rather than an in-app button. Email privacy@jamdailytools.com before deleting if you want a copy of your information.

Deleting your TourneySmith account does not cancel a Google Play or Apple subscription. Public purchasing is not currently enabled, but if you later have a subscription, cancel it in the store first.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to TourneySmith.
  2. Open your own player profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email privacy@jamdailytools.com from the address associated with your sign-in and write “TourneySmith account deletion” in the subject. Tell us whether you used Google, Apple, or Microsoft. Do not send a password or identity document unless we first provide a secure verification method.

We will verify control of the account before acting. This route is also available if you cannot install or sign in to the app.

Want us to delete some of your data but keep your account? Email privacy@jamdailytools.com and tell us what to delete.

What happens immediately

  • You lose access to the account.
  • Your active tournament memberships and organizer roles are removed. Shared tournaments remain for other people.
  • Your saved-player list, reserved player names, block list, and tournament list are removed.
  • Invites and notification delivery tied to the removed memberships stop.

The 30-day recovery period and permanent deletion

For 30 days, the account is marked deleted. Signing in during this period can reactivate the account itself, but does not restore the lists, memberships, roles, reservations, or blocks removed immediately.

After 30 days, authentication, device records, private profile information, tournament history, notifications, and usage counts are scheduled for permanent deletion. Provider backups and security or transaction records can remain for a limited period when technically or legally required.

Subscriptions and app-store billing

Deleting the account or uninstalling the app does not tell Google Play or Apple to cancel billing. If purchasing is enabled in the future, cancel through the store account that made the purchase. Store refund and cancellation rules apply.

What remains for other participants

Tournaments, matches, scores, standings, and results shared with other people are not erased from their event. Completed tournaments keep the name recorded when the event finished. Ongoing records can show “Deleted player.” A placeholder or saved player you created remains only while another player list or tournament still needs that record.

Privacy rights and regional requests

You can request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, information about disclosures, or consent withdrawal when applicable. Brazilian, Mexican, Canadian, Québec, and applicable U.S. rights described in the Privacy Policy remain available. Account deletion is not the only way to exercise a privacy right.

Need help?

Account deletion: privacy@jamdailytools.com. App support: support@jamdailytools.com. Include only the information needed to locate your account.
