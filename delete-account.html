Delete your TourneySmith account

Last updated: September 16, 2026

TourneySmith is made by JAM Daily Tools LLC. Here's how to delete your account, what gets removed, and what stays.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open TourneySmith and sign in.
  2. Go to your own profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

It happens right away. You don't need to hand off or delete your tournaments first.

Delete it without the app

Already uninstalled TourneySmith? Email privacy@jamdailytools.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm it's really your account first. We aim to get back to you within 30 days.

What's removed right away

  • You're taken out of every tournament you organize or play in.
  • Your player list.
  • Your reserved player names.
  • Your blocked list.
  • Your tournament list.

30 days to change your mind

Your account is marked as deleted straight away, but we hold on to it for 30 days. If you sign back in with the same account during that time, it comes back. The things removed right away don't come back, though.

After 30 days we delete your account for good: your sign-in, your devices, your private profile data (like your email and private stats), your tournament history, your notifications, and your usage counts. After that it can't be recovered.

What stays, and why

  • Tournaments you organized. Deleting your account takes you out of them. It doesn't delete them for everyone else. An active tournament with no organizers left gets deleted after 30 days.
  • Match results and scores. Matches you played stay part of the other players' tournaments, with the name you had at the time.
  • Players you created. They stay while a player list or a tournament that's still going uses them, and get deleted once nothing does.
  • Your basic public profile. Right now, the name people saw, your favorite sport, and your stats (if you didn't hide them) aren't removed.
  • Blocks other people placed on you stay during the 30 days, and the other person sees you as a deleted account. After that they're deleted along with your account.

Subscriptions

Purchases aren't turned on in TourneySmith yet. Once they are, keep this in mind: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because your app store bills you, not us. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions before you delete your account, or you'll keep getting charged.
