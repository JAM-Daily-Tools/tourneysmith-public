Delete your TourneySmith account

Last updated: August 10, 2026

TourneySmith is published by JAM Daily Tools LLC. This page explains how to delete your account, what is removed, and what is kept.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open TourneySmith and sign in.
  2. Go to your own profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You do not need to transfer or delete your tournaments first.

Delete without the app

If you have already uninstalled TourneySmith, email privacy@jamdailytools.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before acting. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is removed immediately

  • Your membership and ownership of every tournament you belong to, including your entry in each tournament's member list.
  • Your saved players list.
  • Your reserved placeholder names.
  • Your blocked-users list.
  • Your personal tournament list.

The 30-day recovery window

Your account is marked as deleted straight away, but the underlying record is retained for 30 days. If you sign in again with the same credentials during that window, your account is reactivated. Data that was already removed above is not restored.

After 30 days the account record and your sign-in credentials are permanently deleted, and the account can no longer be recovered.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Tournaments you organised. Deleting your account removes you from them; it does not delete the tournament for everyone else. A tournament left with no remaining organiser is reclaimed automatically once it is no longer in use.
  • Match results and scores. Matches you played remain part of other participants' tournament history.
  • Placeholder players you created. These stay while they are still referenced by a tournament roster, and are reclaimed automatically once nothing references them.
  • Blocks other people placed on you. These are preserved so the block still applies; the other person sees the account as deleted.

Subscriptions

Deleting your TourneySmith account does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are billed by the app store, not by us. Cancel through Google Play → Payments & subscriptions or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions before deleting your account, otherwise billing continues.
