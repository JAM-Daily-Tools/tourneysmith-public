Privacy Policy for TourneySmith

Last updated: October 3, 2026. Document version: 2026-10-03.

1. Scope

This policy covers the TourneySmith mobile app, product website, and invitation pages. TourneySmith is an online tournament service for padel, tennis, pickleball, badminton, squash, racquetball, and table tennis. An account and network connection are required; there is no guest or offline-only mode.

The product website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It can store one language preference in your browser under tourneysmith-language. Cloudflare processes ordinary request information to deliver and protect the website.

3. Information we collect

Account and profile

When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Microsoft, we receive the provider account identifier, email address, and name when the provider supplies one. Apple may provide a relay address when you use Hide My Email. We keep account creation and last sign-in times, the active-device record used to limit simultaneous sessions, accepted Terms version and time, privacy setup state, profile name and optional favorite sport, privacy settings, and notification settings. A profile name is required. We do not ask for a phone number or profile photo.

Players, groups, and tournaments

We store saved player and placeholder names, optional email addresses used to look for an existing account, groups, reserved names, invitations, roles, memberships, and blocks. Tournament records include sport, format, settings, players and teams, schedules, venues, courts, matches, brackets, scores, standings, results, points, notes, statistics, and history. TourneySmith does not send email invitations.

Device, service, and store information

We process notification tokens, device type, app and operating-system versions, crash and error details, account identifiers in server logs, security and integrity signals, feature and rate-limit counts, Remote Config values, and product-usage events. Analytics events use an app-instance identifier and are designed not to contain names, email addresses, phone numbers, player names, or tournament names. AdMob may process an advertising identifier, IP-derived general location, device information, consent state, and ad interactions. If store purchases become available, Google Play or Apple provides product, transaction, subscription, and entitlement status, but not your complete card or bank details.

We do not intentionally request sensitive categories such as health, biometric, religious, political, or union information. Do not place that information in tournament names, notes, or other free-text fields.

4. Why we use information

Our necessary purposes are to create and secure accounts; provide sync, tournaments, scoring, schedules, invitations, roles, profiles, blocks, and notifications; enforce limits; provide support; prevent fraud and abuse; diagnose failures; and meet legal duties. These uses are necessary to perform our agreement, protect the service, or satisfy legal obligations.

We also use limited analytics to understand reliability and feature use. We rely on legitimate interests where permitted and consent where local law requires it. Personalized advertising is optional where consent is required. You can refuse or withdraw that consent through the app’s ad privacy choices without losing the core tournament service.

6. Advertising and analytics choices

The ad-supported experience may show AdMob banners and occasional full-screen ads at natural tournament transitions. Advertising availability and frequency can change through Remote Config. Eligible account entitlements can remove ads, but public purchasing is not enabled as of this policy date.

Where Google’s consent message is required, you can choose personalized or non-personalized advertising and reopen the choices under Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices. Device-level advertising and tracking settings also apply. Under some privacy laws, personalized advertising may be treated as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising even though we do not exchange personal information for money.

7. When information is shared

Tournament organizers and players with access can see the names, roles, schedules, brackets, scores, standings, results, and notes needed for the shared event. A profile can show username, favorite sport, statistics, and tournament history; the Hide stats setting removes statistics and history from other users. Email addresses are not displayed as profile fields. Organizers who typed a player’s email already possess that address.

We share information with the providers listed above, with authorities when law requires it, and in a corporate transaction subject to appropriate notice and protections. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not send marketing email.

9. Retention and account deletion

  • Account and profile information remains while the account is active.
  • Tournaments with no activity for six months can be removed after in-app warnings. An active tournament with no organizer can be removed after 30 days. A tournament with no remaining members can be removed.
  • Profile tournament history can remain after the underlying tournament is removed.
  • Unlinked saved players are removed when no player list or active tournament needs them. Completed tournaments retain their result snapshots.
  • Completed invitations and obsolete memberships are generally cleared about 90 days after they end. Monthly usage counts are retained for up to 13 months.
  • Crash, analytics, advertising, security, and store records follow the configured retention of the relevant provider and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Account deletion immediately removes access, saved-player data, reserved names, the block list, active memberships, and organizer roles. For 30 days, signing in can reactivate the account itself, but it does not restore information removed immediately. After that period, authentication, devices, private profile data, history, notifications, and usage counts are scheduled for permanent deletion. Shared tournament and match records remain for other participants, with the profile reduced to “Deleted player” where needed. See the account deletion page.

10. Your controls

  • Edit your profile name, favorite sport, privacy settings, and notification preferences in the app.
  • Use Hide stats to hide profile statistics and tournament history from other users.
  • Choose who can add you: anyone, only people who know your email, or no one. Accepted tournament invitations can still create the membership you requested.
  • Block or unblock another account. Blocking prevents new invitations and player linking, but does not rewrite tournaments you already share.
  • Choose all, important-only, or muted notifications for each tournament and use system notification settings.
  • Change ad privacy choices when the control is available.
  • Delete your account from the bottom of your profile or submit the web request described on the deletion page.
