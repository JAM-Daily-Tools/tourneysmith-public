Privacy Policy for TourneySmith

Last updated: September 16, 2026

TourneySmith lives at tourneysmith.com.

The short version

TourneySmith ("the App," "we," "us") helps you run racquet sports tournaments: pickleball, padel, tennis, badminton, squash, racquetball, and table tennis. This page covers what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what you can change.

Everything in TourneySmith is stored online with Google Firebase. That's how your tournaments sync across your devices and how everyone in a tournament sees the same brackets and scores. There's no offline-only or guest mode.

Who we are

TourneySmith is run by JAM Daily Tools LLC, a Texas limited liability company. We're the controller of the personal data described here. If you have a question or want to use one of your privacy rights, get in touch:

  • Privacy and data requests: privacy@jamdailytools.com
  • Help with the App: support@jamdailytools.com

What we collect

1. Your account

You sign in with Google, Apple, or Microsoft. From that sign-in we get your name (if the provider shares one), your email address, and an account ID from Firebase Authentication. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, the email we get is Apple's relay address.

We also keep when your account was created, when you last signed in, and which device you're currently signed in on. That last one is how we keep you signed in on one device at a time.

2. Your profile

You can add a username and a favorite sport. Both are optional. We don't collect phone numbers or profile photos.

Your privacy settings (Hide stats and Who can add me) and your notification settings are saved with your account too.

3. People you add

When you add a player, you give us their name and, if you want, their email address. If you only give an email, the App uses the part before the @ as their name until you change it.

We use that email for one thing: checking whether the person has a TourneySmith account. If they do and their settings allow it, they're added to your player list as a contact linked to their account. If not, they're saved as a player with that email so they can be linked later. We never send them an email. The App doesn't send email at all.

You can also put players into groups. Only add people you have a good reason to add, and only the details you actually need.

4. Your tournaments

We store the tournaments you create or join: names, sport, format and settings, players and teams, brackets and courts, schedules, venues and court times, scores, standings, results, stats, match notes, invites, and who the organizers are. When a tournament finishes, we also save a summary of how you did to your profile's tournament history.

5. Stuff we collect automatically

  • Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics): your device model, OS version, app version, what went wrong, and a short log of what the App was doing right before. That log can include your account ID and the names of tournaments or players.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): events like signing in, creating a tournament, adding a player, sending an invite, opening an invite link, blocking someone, tapping upgrade, and pulling to refresh. These are tied to a random ID for your copy of the App, not to your account. We don't put names or email addresses in them, and anything that looks like an email, phone number, or link gets stripped out.
  • Ad data (Google AdMob, free plan only): your device's advertising ID, a rough location based on your IP address, device info, and how you interact with ads.
  • Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): a token for your device, what kind of device it is, and when it was saved, so we can send you notifications. It's removed when you sign out.
  • App integrity checks (Firebase App Check, using Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iOS): these confirm requests are coming from the real App on a real device.
  • Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config): lets us switch a feature off quickly if something breaks.
  • Usage counts and rate limits: how many tournaments, players, invites, links, and groups you create each month, plus short-term counters that slow down abuse. These enforce plan limits.
  • Server logs (Google Cloud Logging): when our backend handles something for you, it logs what happened along with your account ID.

How we use it

  • To run the App and keep your data synced across your devices.
  • To make sharing work: invites, linking players to accounts, and shared tournaments.
  • To send the notifications you've allowed.
  • To enforce plan limits and, once purchases are turned on, handle them.
  • To keep the App safe: blocking, rate limits, and spotting abuse.
  • To fix crashes and bugs.
  • To see which features get used so we can make the App better.
  • To show ads on the free plan.

Who can see your info

Other people in the App

  • People in your tournaments see your name, where you are in the tournament, your scores, standings, and results, and any match notes.
  • Other TourneySmith users who open your profile see your name and favorite sport. They also see your stats and tournament history, unless you turn on Hide stats.
  • Your name is your username if you've set one. If you haven't, it's the name your sign-in provider shared. If there's neither, the App currently shows the part of your email address before the @. Setting a username replaces it.
  • Your full email address isn't shown to other people. If an organizer added you by typing your email, they already have it, and it stays on their player list.
  • Organizers can save you to their player list, depending on your Who can add me setting (see "Your choices" below).

Names and results in a tournament are a snapshot. If you change your username later, tournaments that are still going pick up the new name, but finished tournaments keep the name you had when they finished.

  • Cloud Firestore (Google), where your data lives.

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), for notifications.

  • Google Play and the Apple App Store, for purchases once they're turned on.

Here's Google's Privacy Policy. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't send marketing email.

Purchases

Pro and extra tournament slots aren't for sale yet. When they are, your app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) handles the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details. We just get confirmation of what you bought, like an active Pro plan or slot credits, so we can unlock it and turn off ads.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send them through Firebase Cloud Messaging. They cover things like tournament invites, a tournament starting or finishing, a round wrapping up, your match being ready or its time changing, a new match note, reminders about tournaments that need you (like one that's about to be deleted for inactivity), and plan or billing updates.

You can turn them all off, or pick which kinds you get, under Notification settings in the App. You can also set a single tournament to All notifications, Important only, or Muted. Your device settings work too.

Ads

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Players, Groups, and Tournaments lists, and sometimes a full-screen ad when a tournament starts, a round wraps up, or a tournament finishes. Pro has no ads.

If you're in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your ad consent first. When that applies, you can change your answer later under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

How long we keep things

  • Your account and profile stay as long as your account does.
  • Tournaments with no activity for 6 months get deleted, finished ones included. The App warns you ahead of time, first at about 5 months and again a few days before, and anyone in the tournament can choose to keep it.
  • Tournaments nobody's in anymore get deleted. An active tournament with no organizers left is deleted after 30 days.
  • Your tournament history stays on your profile even if the tournament itself is deleted.
  • Players you've removed, or that got linked to someone's account, are deleted once they're not on anyone's player list and not in a tournament that's still going. Finished tournaments keep the name they had.
  • Finished invites and old tournament memberships are cleared out about 90 days after they end.
  • Monthly usage counts are kept for 13 months.
  • Crash, analytics, and ad data follow Google's retention settings for those services.

When you delete your account

You can delete your account from the bottom of your own profile in the App. Here's what happens:

  • Right away: you're removed from every tournament you organize or play in (the tournaments stay for everyone else), and your player list, reserved player names, blocked list, and tournament list are deleted.
  • For 30 days: your account is marked as deleted. If you sign back in during that time, your account comes back, but the stuff removed right away doesn't.
  • After 30 days: we delete your account, your sign-in, your devices, your private profile data (like your email and private stats), your tournament history, your notifications, and your usage counts.
  • What stays: tournaments and match results other people are part of, with the name you had at the time. Players you created, until nothing uses them anymore. And right now, your basic public profile (the name people saw, your favorite sport, and your stats if you didn't hide them) isn't removed.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel that through Google Play or the App Store. More details are on our account deletion page.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from the bottom of your own profile, as described above.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or to fix, delete, restrict, or move it: email privacy@jamdailytools.com. There's no export button in the App yet, so we handle these by hand. The right still applies, and we'll confirm it's really you first.
  • Hide your stats: turn on Settings → Privacy → Hide stats and other people won't see your stats or tournament history.
  • Choose who can add you: Settings → Privacy → Who can add me has three options:
    • Anyone (the default): people you're in a tournament with can save you to their player list, and anyone who knows your email can add you or link you to a player.
    • Only people who know my email: people have to type your email. Being in a tournament with you isn't enough.
    • No one: nobody can save, add, or link you.
    Tournament invites still work with any setting, since you're the one who accepts them. If you accept an organizer's invite, you'll show up on that organizer's player list whatever this is set to.
  • Block someone: they can't send you new invites or add or link you, and you won't get notifications about what they do. We don't tell them. Tournaments you already share aren't changed. You can see and undo blocks under Settings → Privacy → Blocked users.
  • Change your ad consent: use Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices when it shows up for you, or your device settings (Google → Ads on Android, or tracking settings on iOS). Pro has no ads.
  • Control notifications in the App's Notification settings, per tournament, or in your device settings.

Security

Everything between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access rules limit who can read and change what. Nothing online is 100% secure, but we lean on Google's infrastructure and keep access as tight as we can.

Kids

TourneySmith is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from kids under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, email privacy@jamdailytools.com and we'll delete it.

Some places set a higher age for signing up on your own. In the EEA it's between 13 and 16 depending on the country, and some places go higher. If your local law sets an age above 13, that age applies, and a parent or guardian has to consent for you.

International transfers

We're based in the United States. Your data is processed and stored on Google Cloud, which may be in the United States or other countries.

When personal data moves out of the EEA or the UK, it's covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum where it applies), through Google's Data Processing Addendum for Firebase and Google Cloud. Email privacy@jamdailytools.com if you want more detail.

Complaints

If you're in the EEA or the UK and think we've mishandled your data, we'd like the chance to fix it, so please email privacy@jamdailytools.com. You also have the right to complain to your country's data protection authority, or to the UK Information Commissioner's Office, without contacting us first.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy now and then. When we make a meaningful change, we'll post it here with a new "Last updated" date. If you keep using the App after that, you're accepting the updated policy.
