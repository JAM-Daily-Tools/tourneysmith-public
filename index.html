Built for the work between sign-up and the final score

Set up the event once, then keep organizers, players, and viewers on the same tournament.

Choose the format and scoring

Run single elimination, double elimination, round robin, ladder, or King of the Court. Configure sets and games, tie-breaks, win by two, point caps, rotating partners, consolation brackets, and points rankings where the format supports them.

Bring back the right players

Reuse saved players, add placeholder players, organize player groups, and select from as many as four groups when building a tournament. TourneySmith supports singles and doubles where each sport allows them.

Share one live tournament

Invite players, viewers, and co-organizers. Shared tournaments keep live brackets, standings, results, and score entry together so everyone follows the same event.

Plan the day

Schedule the tournament, set venues and courts, and control tournament notifications. Each person can choose all notifications, important updates only, or mute a tournament.

Keep the sporting record

Player profiles bring together sport statistics, wins, losses, win rate, and tournament history without changing the results stored in completed events.

Move between devices

Sign in on Android or iOS and cloud sync keeps your tournaments available. Privacy controls let you hide statistics, choose who can add you, block users, and delete your account.
